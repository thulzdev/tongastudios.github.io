Cirsus: The Chaos Arena may use third-party services that collect information automatically when you use the game.

Google Play Games Services, for features such as player authentication and leaderboards.

Cirsus: The Chaos Arena may use Google Play Games Services to provide features such as Google Play Games sign-in and leaderboards.
